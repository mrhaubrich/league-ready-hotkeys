# Scoring

RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort.

Reach is 5 for core interaction, 3 for lifecycle, 1 for maintenance. Impact is 3 essential, 2 major, 1 useful, 0.5 minor. Confidence is 1 proven, 0.8 well understood, 0.6 uncertain. Effort is focused engineer-days. Recalculate when scope, dependencies, or evidence changes; never use score to bypass a hard lock or validation gate.

The approved matrix is in `README.md`. Validation tasks remain required even when implementation compiles.
